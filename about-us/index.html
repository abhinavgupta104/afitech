About AFI Technologies

We make the part of your equipment that every user touches. For 15 years, from a workshop in Kondhwa, Pune.

AFI Technologies manufactures membrane keypads, graphic overlays and PCB-backed switch panels for equipment builders. Our parts end up on ventilators and weighing terminals, on crane load indicators and laboratory analysers — the interface between a machine and the person operating it.

It is a component most buyers think about late and then need quickly. It is also the part that determines whether an instrument feels well made. A keypad that goes vague after two years, or a legend that wipes off under disinfectant, undoes a great deal of careful engineering behind the panel.

So the work is mostly detail: choosing a dome that still snaps cleanly after a million presses, compensating printed artwork for the stretch that embossing introduces, matching a grey to a bezel moulded by someone else five years ago. We do that work in-house — screen printing, die-cutting, embossing, dome placement, assembly and inspection all happen in the same building.

How we work

Four things we hold ourselves to

  • We tell you what will not work

    An emboss too deep for the key size, a dome force wrong for the duty cycle, a window ink too dense for the LED behind it — these are cheap to fix on a drawing and expensive to fix in a tool. We raise them before quoting, not after.

  • One part number, one supplier

    Overlay, switch and rigid board are made in the same building under one quality system. Sourcing them together removes the interfaces where first-article problems usually appear.

  • The specification is public

    Our ratings, materials and actuation figures are published on the site so you can rule us in or out before spending time on an enquiry.

  • Built to your drawing, not adapted from a catalogue

    Every panel is made for the equipment it goes into. There is no standard part we try to talk you into.
