Enquiry

Request a quote

Tell us what you need made. The more of the specification you can give us now, the closer the first number will be to the final one.

Feedback required

PDF, DXF, DWG, STEP or a photo, up to 10 MB. A drawing gets you a firm price instead of an estimate.

We reply to most enquiries within one working day. Your drawing is treated as confidential and is not shared outside AFI Technologies.

What to send

The more of this you include, the closer the first number will be to the final one.

  • Panel outline dimensions, and the key layout — a PDF, DXF or STEP drawing is ideal
  • Number of keys, and which of them need tactile feedback
  • Whether LEDs or a display window are required, and where
  • Connector type and tail exit position
  • Colours: Pantone or RAL references, or a physical sample to match
  • Environment: temperature range, sealing requirement, any chemicals present
  • Annual quantity and the quantity for the first order

No drawing yet? Send a photo of the panel you are replacing, or a sketch with dimensions. We will work from either.

What happens next

  1. 01

    We read the drawing

    Before quoting we check emboss heights against key sizes, dome force against your duty cycle, and window ink density against the LED you have specified.

  2. 02

    We come back with questions, or a price

    If something in the drawing will cause trouble at tooling, you hear about it now rather than at first article. Most enquiries are answered within one working day.

  3. 03

    Artwork approval, then first article

    Artwork goes back and forth until you sign it off. Tooling follows approval, and a first article is produced for your inspection before the run.
