Switch and connector options

Both are decisions worth settling at drawing stage rather than at first article.

Switch options

Metal dome
Stainless steel domes in 4-leg or round formats, 250–400 g. Positive snap, 1 million+ cycles.
Polyester dome
A formed dome in the circuit film. Softer feedback than steel, at lower cost and with longer life.
Feather-touch non-tactile
No dome at all. The lightest actuation force and the longest life of any construction we build.

Connector systems

Flextail
The printed tail runs directly into an FFC or ZIF socket on your board. Lowest cost, lowest profile.
Crimpflex
A crimped pin header applied to the tail, mating with a standard housing. Field-serviceable.
Male solder tab
Tinned tabs for direct soldering where a connector would be another failure point.

Before you enquire

What we need to quote

A drawing gets you a firm price. Without one we can still estimate, but the number will carry more caveats than either of us wants.

  1. 01 Panel outline dimensions, and the key layout — a PDF, DXF or STEP drawing is ideal
  2. 02 Number of keys, and which of them need tactile feedback
  3. 03 Whether LEDs or a display window are required, and where
  4. 04 Connector type and tail exit position
  5. 05 Colours: Pantone or RAL references, or a physical sample to match
  6. 06 Environment: temperature range, sealing requirement, any chemicals present
  7. 07 Annual quantity and the quantity for the first order

Common questions

What is the minimum order quantity?
There is no fixed minimum. Tooling is the practical constraint: a first order of 25–50 pieces is common for a new panel, and unit cost drops steeply above a few hundred. Tell us your annual volume and the first order separately and we will quote both.
How long does the first article take?
Artwork and drawing approval usually takes a few days of back-and-forth. Once approved, a first article typically follows within two to three weeks depending on tooling. Repeat orders are faster. Confirm current lead times with your enquiry.
Can you match an existing keypad?
Yes, and sending a physical sample is far more reliable than sending a specification. A sample lets us measure the dome force, match the colour under the right finish, and copy the tail and connector exactly.

Does this fit your application?

If a specification here rules us in, send the drawing. If something rules us out, tell us what — the list above is not the limit of what we can build.
